Every model, texture and sound in Corley's Island

The machines, boats, things and trees are exported from the running game, so each one is the exact set of meshes and materials the game draws. Tap one to turn it round and see its wireframe. Every bin sorts: by triangles, size, file size, length and more.

Machines and boats

Everything you can drive or ride, plus the boats on the lake, the crane barge and the worker. The Graphics setting's Machine models picks Detailed (the glTF models with photo-textured paint, steel and rubber) or Simple (the shapes built in code, used on Low graphics).

Things to place

The map editor's Add catalogue. Each kind is one model drawn as an instanced mesh, so a hundred fence sections cost one draw. Max is how many of that kind the game draws.

Trees

The five species at full detail, with the far level of detail the game swaps to at range, and the bark and leaf textures they share. Leaves are grey in the texture; each tree tints them.

Tree textures

Compare all models

Every 3D asset in one table. Tap a column to sort by it, tap a row to open the model. Draws are the separate meshes the GPU is asked to draw for one copy.

Material library

What the map editor's Paint tool lays on the ground and blocks (MAT_LIST in materials.js). Tile size is how many feet one repeat of the texture covers. PBR marks the ones with relief and sheen maps for High graphics.

Natural ground

The undug island surface in three resolution tiers (the Graphics setting picks one), its normal maps, the macro tint, and the looks painted mud takes as it dries.

Machine textures

The photo detail the Detailed machines wear: paint, rubber and bare steel, each with relief and roughness maps.
