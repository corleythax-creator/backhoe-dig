Out to the borders, and up

Today's world (the white dashed line) is all lake down the middle with land only north and south. The proposals fill its empty sides with shore work, link them with one road round the lake, and send a haul road up the mountain behind town.

Proposals

Sizes are in feet from the map; heights on the mountains are the painting's, to be settled when the ground is built. Hours are one experienced three.js developer working by hand. The concept pictures are AI-generated mood pictures, not the layout. Pick any by number and they go on the idea board.
