Dig Anywhere Plans

Today only a 210 ft square around the gas main can be dug. This plan splits the whole map into 105 ft tiles that become diggable the first time a bucket touches them, so every hole stays where you left it while the phone still draws no more than it does now.

Ground drawn from the game's own heights (sampled every 13 ft). North up, to scale, feet from the mesa top's centre. Builds on idea D14 "Dig anywhere" on the idea board.

Recommendation: tiles

Only tiles let the whole map be dug and keep every hole at full detail. Today's square becomes the first four tiles, so the game plays exactly as it does now before any new ground opens. D14 on its own is cheaper, but leaving a site would blur its holes into the coarse ground.

Sheet 3

Build phases

Each phase ships as its own version and leaves the game playable.

  1. Grid becomes tiles12 hDone
    The dig grid gets a position per tile instead of always sitting at the trench. That means fixing the 59 places in 14 files (water, mud, rain, dirt, editor, mower, truck, trench box and others) that assume the square is centred on the mesa. Today's square becomes tiles E0/W0 by N0/S0, and nothing visible changes.
  2. Ground systems on every tile10 hDone
    Puddles and seepage, how wet the soil is and how wet it got, cracks, spoil colour, mud, paint and the trench box each move to per-tile storage and cross tile edges cleanly. Ruts on a dug tile become real dug ground; the 26 ft mud tiles stay for ground that has never been dug.
  3. Loading and far tiles6 hDone (4 tiles on every setting for now)
    A tile is made the first time a bucket, blade or tyre digs it. Tiles near the machine get the full mesh; the rest are drawn at 2 ft detail from their saved shape. The Graphics setting caps the full-detail count: 4 on Low, 9 on Medium and High.
  4. Saves5 hDone
    Each tile saves only the points that changed, as the save does now. An old save loads into the four trench tiles, so no hole is lost, and saves still sync between phone and PC.
  5. Editor and things on the ground6 hDone
    The map editor refreshes every tile when it changes the ground. Digging into a road lowers it, trees on dug ground fall, buildings and bridge ends can't be dug under, and the islands' own ground joins the tiles.
  6. Testing and frame rate4 h
    Headless checks of digging across a tile edge, loading a save, and driving between islands; a frame rate check on the phone on Low, which must stay above 30 FPS.
Sheet 4

Where digging opens

Step 1: Corley's · 43 tiles

The mesa top, the ramp, the Lower Bench and the cliffs and beaches round them. Opens with phases 1 to 4.

Step 2: the islands · 81 tiles

Family Island 42, Dexter's 19, Jessica's 20. Dexter's yard is the natural second dig site, with the machines already parked there. Opens with phase 5.

Later: the mainland · about 400 tiles

The town, hayfield and woods are drawn on a coarse 6.5 ft mesh today. They need finer ground first, so they wait for a separate plan.

What stays the same

  • The gas main, the trench and today's holes stay exactly where they are.
  • You can still dig at most 11.5 ft down. Digging deeper is idea D11, a separate change.
  • The phone loads no more ground than it does today.
  • Map edits still change the ground under undug tiles at once.

Risks to watch

  • Water and mud crossing tile edges without seams or jumps.
  • Memory on long sessions: each loaded tile is about 5 MB, so tiles far from the machine are dropped back to their saved shape.
  • Save size if a player digs everywhere: about 160 KB per fully dug tile before compression.
