Corley's Island · planning record

Four Islands: three layout ideas

One large Family Island in the middle, three personal mesas around it (Corley's, the one you play on now, plus Dexter's and Jessica's), bridges and docks between them, and one long bridge heading off across the lake toward the mainland.

All three ideas leave Corley's Island exactly where and as it is, and it stays the only island with digging, mud and the gas main. Dexter's and Jessica's start as flat mesas the same size as Corley's (about 295 ft across, cliffs about 150 ft to the water). Maps are to scale, north up, in feet.

Family IslandCentral and largest: about 395 to 525 ft across, the meeting place and the start of the mainland bridge

What goes on each island

Family Island

  • A big timber family house with a wraparound porch facing the lake
  • An open lawn with a picnic pavilion, fire pit and string lights
  • A flagpole and a lookout at the north rim facing Corley's
  • A stair down the cliff to the docks: a boathouse, a pontoon boat and kayaks
  • A roundabout where all the bridges meet, and the gate to the mainland bridge

Dexter's Island

  • Flat gravel top, graded so machines can go anywhere
  • An equipment shed with roll-up doors and a fuel tank
  • Parked machines: a skid steer, a small dozer, a wheel loader and a lowboy trailer (each one drivable later, one at a time)
  • A practice yard of stockpiles and cones
  • A wide steel bridge rated for machines, so Dexter's equipment can drive to Corley's dig

Jessica's Island

  • Dense pine and leafy forest with a winding gravel footpath and lanterns
  • A stone and timber cottage with a mossy roof and a smoking chimney
  • A library inside: shelves floor to ceiling, a rolling ladder, a window seat, armchairs by the fireplace, a reading lamp
  • Warm window glow at dusk, a herb garden and a bench at the cliff edge
  • Machines park where the forest starts; the path to the cottage is on foot
How it gets built

Notes for the code

Ground

  • groundHeight grows a list of islands (centre, size, top height). Corley's mesa math stays as it is; the new ones are flat mesas with the same cliff profile.
  • The 0.5 m dig grid and editor layers stay on Corley's. The new islands get a coarse ground mesh and their own editor layer later.
  • Bridge decks are surfaces for surfAt(), so walking, driving and blocks all work on them.

Phone speed

  • Far islands draw as low-detail meshes with merged, instanced trees; full detail only for the island you are on.
  • The library interior loads as you walk to the cottage door.
  • Shadows stay near the player (as now), so the extra islands cost little on Low graphics.

Saves, maps and editor

  • Machines on Dexter's join the save list and WK_RIDES as they become drivable.
  • The minimap and big map zoom out to show all four islands.
  • Trees and buildings on the new islands are placed assets, so the map editor can move them.
Recommendation

Build Idea C, in three steps

Idea C (the Gateway Line) gives each person a bridge that suits them: a heavy truss for Corley's trucks, a machine-rated steel bridge for Dexter, a covered wooden footbridge for Jessica. It also puts the mainland bridge in a straight line through the Family Island, which is where B01 wants to go next.

Steps: (1) the Family Island and the bridge from Corley's, with the mainland bridge stub; (2) Dexter's mesa, shed and parked machines; (3) Jessica's forest, cottage and library. Each step is a release on its own.

Chosen Idea C was picked. Step 1 shipped in v74: the Family Island, the truss bridge from the Lower Bench and the first stretch of the mainland bridge. It stops at a barrier about 335 ft out, with bare piers and a crane beyond, because the mountains begin about 1,540 ft from Corley's. The docks come later.

Built Jessica's Island shipped in v75, ahead of Dexter's: the red covered footbridge, the forest, the herb garden, the cliff bench and the cottage with its library.

Bridges from Corley's leave from the Lower Bench (24 ft below the top), so they run level to the new islands. Idea A is the better pick if the islands should feel equal and grand; Idea B if you want the islands close enough to see each other's work and drive a loop.
