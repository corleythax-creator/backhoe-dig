Mesa Layout Plans

Three ways to grow the mesa to 1.5 times its size. Each one mixes grass, worked dirt and haul roads, adds hills, and gives the machines a road down to a lower level. The maps below are drawn from the same terrain formulas the game would use, so the heights, grades and areas are measured, not guessed.

Common to all three

  • The mesa grows from about 197 ft across to about 295 ft across (1.5×). The extra ground goes south, east and west.
  • The north rim stays where it is, so the trench, the gas main, the tall-grass patch, the start spots and the truck's dump-over-the-cliff spot don't move.
  • The north rim road stays as the dump road. The single ring road is replaced by a road network: haul roads at 12 ft wide and narrower dirt tracks at 8 ft.
  • The cliffs still drop about 150 ft to the meadows and lake on every side the plan doesn't change.

Reading the maps

North is up. Thin lines are contours every 5 ft, heavier every 25 ft. The dashed white line is today's mesa rim. The red mark is the trench, the yellow dashes are the gas main and the outlined box is the tall-grass patch. The section line A–A′ is the cut shown in the profile under each map.

Shared groundwork

What changes in the code for any plan

Terrain

  • EDGE goes from 30 m to 45 m and the mesa centre MZ moves from 16 to 30, so the north rim stays put.
  • baseHeight gains the plan's hills, levels and graded roads. The 210 ft dig grid stays where it is, around the trench.
  • The rest of the mesa needs its own ground mesh at about 1.6 ft spacing (cut out around the dig grid, like the outer ground is now). Hills and road edges look blocky on the 6.5 ft outer mesh.

Roads and driving

  • roadOff and roadTint change from one ring to a list of road centrelines, each with a height profile for the graded ramps.
  • The four "stay on the mesa" checks (excavator, truck, mower, blocks) use one shared test of driveable ground instead of the distance from the centre, so the lower levels count as ground and the cliff edges still stop the machines.
  • The truck already rolls back on a grade; it needs a check that it can climb a loaded ramp in low gear.

Scenery and maps

  • Grass tufts spread over the bigger area: about 14,000 instead of 9,000 to keep the same density, and none on dirt or roads.
  • Tree placement learns about hills and the lower levels.
  • The minimap and big map pick up the new roads and shade the hills; the big map's 790 ft view already covers the bigger mesa.
