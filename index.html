Backhoe Dig

Work order

Dig a 3 ft wide trench along the white paint, 3 ft deep. A gas main crosses at the yellow paint about 4 ft down. Load the spoil into the dump truck and haul it off. Expose it. Don't hit it.

Left stick
Swing left/right. Push up to reach the stick out, pull down to bring it in.
Right stick
Left curls the bucket in (scoop), right dumps. Push up for boom down, pull for boom up.
Drive
Tap Drive and the left stick moves the tracks.
Truck
Swing a full bucket over the truck bed and dump. It starts parked on the dirt road that loops the mesa. Tap Truck to drive it: left stick steers and gives gas, right stick raises the bed. Pull back to brake; hold it at a stop to shift into reverse. A full load is heavy: it pulls slowly, stops long and leans in turns. Back up to the cliff edge and dump it over to haul it off, or take the ramp down the scarp and dump it in the Lower Bench yard.
Mow
A patch of tall grass grows north of the trench, with a zero-turn mower parked beside it. Tap Mow: each stick is a lap bar. Left stick up/down drives the left wheel, right stick up/down the right wheel. Both up goes straight, one up and one back spins it in place; the left stick sideways also turns it. The blades run in Mow mode. Each pass lays the grass over the way you drove: it looks light when you look the same way and dark when you look against it, so the stripes swap as you walk around. The grass grows back: about a minute after a pass it starts coming up, and in about five minutes it is tall again, so a lawn left too long needs mowing again. Your mowing is saved with the rest of the site (it only grows while the game runs), and Reset leaves it mowed.
Soil
Dark topsoil, then red clay that holds a steep wall. Sand means the gas main is close, and it slumps. Grey rock is past trench depth and slow going. Dug dirt fluffs up about a quarter, so it fills the truck faster than the hole it came from.
Cave-ins
Walls deeper than about 5 ft, or with spoil piled within 2 ft of the edge, cave in after a while. Dirt trickling off the lip means get the bucket out.
Groundwater
Below about 6 ft the hole meets the water table and slowly fills with muddy water. Dirt dug from under it comes up as mud: it sticks in the bucket and truck bed, and wet spoil bogs the truck and slows the tracks. Tap Pump (or P) to dewater the hole.
Trench box
Swing the bucket over the yellow box and press T (controller X, or the Hook box button). It hangs from the bucket and turns with the swing. Lower it into the trench and press again to set it. Walls between its panels can't slump or cave in, so dig deep inside it and leapfrog it along the trench.
Walk
Tap Walk (or press 5) to get out and walk around as the site worker. The left stick walks the way the camera faces; push it all the way to jog. The right stick turns the camera and looks up and down. Tap Jump (Space) to jump: you can jump onto a 1 ft block, a 2 ft one or a 3 ft one, and up a stack one block at a time. Trench walls are too steep to walk up, so jump out. Cab view looks through his eyes.
Blocks
Tap Blocks (or press B) and pick a size: 1×1, 2×2 or 3×3 ft concrete cubes. A yellow outline shows where one lands under the bucket, or right in front of you in Walk mode; tap Place (Space, or E in Walk mode) to set it. They snap to a 1 ft grid and stack. Lift (X) picks up the top block under the bucket, or the one in front of you. Tracks and tires roll over a 1 ft block; taller ones stop them.
Look
Drag the scene to look around.
Map
Tap the minimap or press M for the big map.
Screen
Tap the status card to fold it down. Speed test, Reset, Sound (on/off and volume sliders), Graphics (Low for phones, Medium for laptops, High for desktop PCs; each device remembers its own), UI size (1x, 1.5x, 2x) and Dirt detail (1x, 2x, 4x, 16x: more clods, flying dirt and lumpier loads; 16x is for screenshots and fast computers, turn it down if the game slows) are in the ☰ menu. The site is saved in this browser: the dig, the dumped dirt, the excavator, the truck and its load, the trench box, blocks and the mowing are all still there when you come back. Reset starts fresh ground.
Controller
Sticks work like the two on-screen sticks. Triggers are gas and reverse when driving. LB / RB switch machine · Y cab view · A place block (in Walk mode A jumps and X places) · D-pad up blocks on/off, left/right block size, down pick up · View map · Menu this help · B closes.
Keys
WASD (or arrows) is always the left stick, IJKL is always the right stick, in every mode. 1 Dig · 2 Drive · 3 Truck · 4 Mow · 5 Walk (Tab cycles). Hold Shift for slow, fine moves. C cab view · M map · P pump · N sound on/off · H this help. Blocks: B on/off · Space place · V size · X pick up. Walk: Space jump · E place block.

To scoop: boom down until the teeth bite, curl in while pulling the stick toward you, boom up, swing, then dump.
