Corley's Island

Left stick
Swing left/right. Push up to reach the stick out, pull down to bring it in.
Right stick
Left curls the bucket in (scoop), right dumps. Push up for boom down, pull for boom up.
Drive
Tap Drive and the left stick moves the tracks.
Truck
Swing a full bucket over the truck bed and dump. It starts parked on the dirt road that loops the mesa. Tap Truck to drive it: left stick steers and gives gas, right stick raises the bed. Pull back to brake; hold it at a stop to shift into reverse. A full load is heavy: it pulls slowly, stops long and leans in turns. Back up to the cliff edge and dump it over to haul it off, or take the ramp down the scarp and dump it in the Lower Bench yard.
Mow
A patch of tall grass grows north of the trench, with a zero-turn mower parked beside it. Tap Mow: each stick is a lap bar. Left stick up/down drives the left wheel, right stick up/down the right wheel. Both up goes straight, one up and one back spins it in place; the left stick sideways also turns it. On a keyboard, W and S drive it straight and A and D turn it; the number pad works the lap bars: 8 and 2 both forward or back, 4 and 6 spin in place, 7 and 1 the left bar, 9 and 3 the right bar. The blades run in Mow mode. Each pass lays the grass over the way you drove: it looks light when you look the same way and dark when you look against it, so the stripes swap as you walk around. The grass grows back: about a minute after a pass it starts coming up, and in about five minutes it is tall again, so a lawn left too long needs mowing again. Your mowing is saved with the rest of the site (it only grows while the game runs), and Reset leaves it mowed.
Soil
Dark topsoil, then red clay that holds a steep wall. Sand means the gas main is close, and it slumps. Grey rock is past trench depth and slow going. Dug dirt fluffs up about a quarter, so it fills the truck faster than the hole it came from.
Cave-ins
Walls deeper than about 5 ft, or with spoil piled within 2 ft of the edge, cave in after a while. Dirt trickling off the lip means get the bucket out.
Groundwater
Below about 6 ft the hole meets the water table and slowly fills with muddy water. Dirt dug from under it comes up as mud: it sticks in the bucket and truck bed, and wet spoil bogs the truck and slows the tracks. Tap Pump (or P) to dewater the hole.
Mud
Paint Mud anywhere on the mesa with the map editor's Paint tool to lay soaked ground. Tracks and tires sink up to 9 in and squeeze the mud into ragged berms, and the ruts fill with puddles. Mud slows the machines and coats them, and pushing on in it digs them in until the tracks or tires spin and throw mud. Stuck? Rock it free: forward, then back, then forward again. On Low graphics the puddles are drawn more simply and less mud flies; the mud itself is the same on every device.
Rain
Showers roll through now and then (☰ then Weather: Auto, or Rain to keep it raining, or Dry). Rain soaks the ground: dug dirt, dirt yards, clay and topsoil turn to mud first, dirt roads and grass get soft, sand and gravel drain, and rock and paving stay hard. Low ground soaks fastest. Rain fills holes and ruts with puddles. When it stops the ground dries over several minutes (sand fast, clay slow) and the puddles drain away; it keeps drying while the game is closed. Phones on Low graphics draw fewer raindrops; the rain and the mud it makes are the same on every device.
Trench box
Swing the bucket over the yellow box and press T (controller X, or the Hook box button). It hangs from the bucket and turns with the swing. Lower it into the trench and press again to set it. Walls between its panels can't slump or cave in, so dig deep inside it and leapfrog it along the trench.
Walk
Tap Walk (or press 5) to get out and walk around as the site worker. The left stick walks the way the camera faces; push it all the way to jog, and hold Shift (controller: click the left stick) to sprint; on a touch screen tap Sprint, which stays on until you stop. The right stick turns the camera and looks up and down. Tap Jump (Space) to jump about 6 ft: onto any block, and up a stack two blocks at a time. Trench walls are too steep to walk up, so jump out. Cab view looks through his eyes. Walk up to the excavator, the dump truck or the mower and tap Get in (F) to climb in and drive it; press F (or Get out) again to climb out beside it.
Blocks
Tap Blocks (or press B) and pick a size: 1×1, 2×2 or 3×3 ft concrete cubes. A yellow outline shows where one lands under the bucket, or right in front of you in Walk mode; tap Place (E) to set it. They snap to a 1 ft grid and stack. Lift (X) picks up the top block under the bucket, or the one in front of you. Tracks and tires roll over a 1 ft block; taller ones stop them.
Family Island
A steel truss bridge runs from the Lower Bench, south across the water, to the Family Island: the family house with its porch, a picnic pavilion, a fire pit, the flagpole and a roundabout. Drive any machine or walk across. The roads west and east stop where Dexter's and Jessica's bridges will go, and the mainland bridge to the south is still being built.
Look
Drag the scene to look around.
Map
Tap the minimap or press M for the big map.
Map editor
☰ menu, then Map editor (or G). The game pauses while you edit. Raise, Lower, Flatten and Smooth reshape the ground under a round brush; Dirt and Grass paint it. Paint lays a material from the library (gravel, concrete, steel plate, brick, wood and more) under a round or square brush; Natural ground clears it, and digging cuts through it. Select picks a tree, boulder or machine: drag it to move it, and turn, resize, copy or delete it on its card. Add places pine trees, leafy trees or boulders. Query reads the height, slope and surface of any spot. Roads lays out the road network: tap a road to pick it, drag its dots to move them, tap the picked road between dots to add one, or tap New road and lay one point by point. Its card sets the width, grass up the middle and grading (cut and filled to a height at each point, like the ramp) and deletes points or the road. Look moves the view: drag, pinch to zoom, twist to turn (mouse: right button turns, wheel zooms; keys WASD, Q E, R F). Undo and Redo step through your edits. The map saves on this device as you edit; Map file copies it as text to keep or share, loads a copied map, or starts over.
Screen
Tap the status card to fold it down. Speed test, Reset, Sound (on/off and volume sliders), Graphics (Low for phones, Medium for laptops, High for desktop PCs; each device remembers its own), UI size (1x, 1.5x, 2x) and Dirt detail (1x, 2x, 4x, 16x: more clods, flying dirt and lumpier loads; 16x is for screenshots and fast computers, turn it down if the game slows) are in the ☰ menu. The site is saved in this browser: the dig, the dumped dirt, the excavator, the truck and its load, the trench box, blocks and the mowing are all still there when you come back. Reset starts fresh ground. To play the same site on your phone and computer, open ☰ then Sync devices: get a code on one and type it on the other.
Controller
Sticks work like the two on-screen sticks. Triggers are gas and reverse when driving. LB / RB switch machine · Y cab view · A place block (in Walk mode A jumps, X places and a left stick click sprints) · D-pad up blocks on/off, left/right block size, down pick up · View map · Menu this help · B closes, or gets you in and out of machines.
Keys
Every key can be changed under Keys in the ☰ menu, which also lists what each one does. The standard keys: WASD (or arrows) is the left stick and IJKL the right stick, in every mode but Mow. Mow: W S drive straight, A D turn, and the number pad works the lap bars (8 2 both, 4 6 spin in place, 7 1 left bar, 9 3 right bar). 1 Dig · 2 Drive · 3 Truck · 4 Mow · 5 Walk (Tab cycles). Hold Shift for slow, fine moves in a machine (on foot it sprints). C cab view · M map · P pump · N sound on/off · H this help. Blocks: B on/off · E place · V size · X pick up. Walk: Space jump · Shift sprint · E place block · F get in and out.

To scoop: boom down until the teeth bite, curl in while pulling the stick toward you, boom up, swing, then dump.
